Runners are interchangeable

The unit of AFK work is the agent session: one fresh context window working one ticket from its brief to its evidence. Symphony, the triage cron, or a person's terminal are interchangeable ways of launching one — which is why the process record never names them. A process that depends on a particular orchestrator is a process you cannot replace parts of.

The two runner pages on this site name products because runners are real and worth showing. But notice the direction of the dependency: the runners read the process; the process never reads the runners. Swap the dispatcher tomorrow and every page except its own stays true.

One consequence: board comments and PR descriptions stay contract-voiced no matter which runner launched the session that wrote them. Personality belongs to conversational agents; the tracker gets the same colleague's voice from everyone.
